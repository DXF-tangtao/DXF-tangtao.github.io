About Me & This Site

关于我与本站

Hi, I’m Tang — Writing from Chongqing, China

Hello, I’m Tang.

I was born and raised in Chongqing, one of the most visually unique and futuristic cities in China.

For many years, I worked as a software engineer, spending my days around code, servers, and technology. Outside work, I always enjoyed walking through the streets of Chongqing, watching how quickly this city — and China itself — was changing.

This website is my personal way of documenting those changes.

Not through official narratives or polished tourism marketing, but through the perspective of someone who actually lives here.

I simply want to show a more real, modern, and human side of China.

Chongqing — A City That Feels Like Cyberpunk

The first time many foreign travelers visit Chongqing, they usually say the same thing:

“This city looks like a sci-fi movie.”

And honestly, I understand why.

Chongqing is built between mountains and rivers. Roads overlap across multiple levels. Light rail trains pass directly through residential buildings. Entire neighborhoods climb vertically along steep hillsides.

At night, neon reflections spread across the Yangtze and Jialing Rivers, while thousands of lights cover the mountains like a giant cyberpunk landscape.

Even navigation apps sometimes struggle here.

But what fascinates me most about Chongqing is not only its futuristic appearance.

It’s the contrast.

You can walk out of a futuristic shopping mall filled with robots and AI screens, then immediately enter an old alley where people still play mahjong, drink tea, and eat hotpot late into the night.

That mix of technology, chaos, warmth, and everyday life feels uniquely Chinese.

And that is the Chongqing I want to share with the world.

Why I Started This Website

Over the years, I noticed something interesting.

A lot of international content about China feels incomplete or outdated.

Some articles only focus on politics.
Some still describe China as if it were ten years ago.
Others completely ignore how fast daily life in China is changing.

But modern China evolves incredibly fast.

Today, in many Chinese cities:

  • Mobile payment is completely normal
  • High-speed rail connects most major regions
  • Delivery robots work inside malls and hotels
  • AI tools are becoming part of daily life
  • Electric vehicles fill the streets
  • Cities are becoming smarter every year

At the same time, traditional street life and local culture still remain deeply alive.

That contrast fascinates me.

So instead of writing as a tourist visiting China briefly, I wanted to write from the perspective of someone who actually lives here.

Not to “promote” China.

Just to document the real version of it.

What You’ll Find Here

This website mainly focuses on two things.

1. China Travel & Urban Culture

I write about:

  • Cities worth visiting in China
  • Chongqing local life
  • Night photography and cyberpunk cityscapes
  • Chinese street culture
  • Hidden places most tourists never see
  • Real travel experiences from a local perspective

Instead of traditional travel guides, I care more about how a city actually feels.

Its rhythm.
Its people.
Its atmosphere at midnight.
Its ordinary daily life.

Because I believe those details tell a much more authentic story about China.

2. China’s AI, Robotics & Future Technology

As a former software engineer, I’m also deeply interested in how technology is reshaping modern China.

Here, I write about:

  • AI development in China
  • Robotics competitions and innovation
  • Smart transportation
  • EVs and digital life
  • How technology changes travel and daily life

But I try to explain these things in simple language.

Not like a tech company.
Not like corporate media.

Just from the perspective of an ordinary person living inside this rapidly changing environment.

3. Global Hot Events Sharing & Personal Views

I keep track of real-time hot topics, international current affairs and cross-border information around the world all the time.

In addition to technology and travel content, I will sort out popular international events, sort out the complete context of events in plain language, and share objective and rational personal opinions and independent thinking.

I dig deep into the causes, development trends and long-term impacts behind events, view different voices in a neutral and inclusive attitude, helping overseas readers fully understand the world situation and build a diverse and rational global vision.

The China I See

Modern China is difficult to describe with a single label.

It is ancient and futuristic at the same time.

One moment, you might walk through a centuries-old alley.
A few minutes later, you could enter a fully automated shopping mall filled with robots and AI systems.

That coexistence of old and new feels incredibly unique.

And honestly, I think the outside world still hasn’t fully seen this side of China yet.

That is one of the reasons I keep writing.

Final Words

I do not want this website to feel like corporate media or polished tourism marketing.

I want it to feel personal, honest, and real.

If you are interested in:

  • China travel
  • Cyberpunk-style cities
  • Urban photography
  • AI and future technology
  • Modern Chinese life
  • Futuristic Asian urban culture

Then welcome.

I hope this website helps you discover a side of China that feels more human, more modern, and more real than you expected.

“Real stories are always more powerful than stereotypes.”

关于我与本站

你好，我是 Tang（唐）。

我出生并生活在中国重庆，一座经常被外国游客称为“现实版赛博朋克”的城市。

很多年里，我是一名软件工程师，每天和代码、服务器以及各种技术打交道。
但工作之外，我更喜欢在重庆的大街小巷里闲逛，观察这座城市，以及整个中国，正在发生的变化。

于是，我创建了这个网站。

这里没有官方式宣传，也没有刻意包装。
我只是想用一个普通重庆人的视角，记录真实的中国。

重庆：一座不像现实世界的城市

很多外国游客第一次来到重庆时，都会说一句类似的话：

“这地方像科幻电影。”

而重庆，确实容易给人这种感觉。

这是一座建立在山脉与江河之间的巨大城市。

这里有：

  • 穿楼而过的轻轨
  • 层层叠叠的立体道路
  • 永远起伏的山城地形
  • 被霓虹灯照亮的江面
  • 深夜依旧热闹的火锅店
  • 能让导航软件“迷路”的复杂结构

但真正让我着迷的，并不仅仅是它的“未来感”。

而是它强烈的反差。

你可能刚从一座布满 AI 屏幕和机器人服务的现代商场走出来，几分钟后，又进入一条老街，看到老人打麻将、喝茶、吃火锅。

科技、高楼、烟火气、人情味，在这里同时存在。

这种感觉，非常“中国”，也非常“重庆”。

而这正是我想记录下来的东西。

为什么创建这个网站

这些年，我发现海外互联网上关于中国的很多内容，其实并不完整。

有些内容只关注政治。
有些停留在“古老东方”的刻板印象。
还有很多信息，仿佛还停留在十年前。

但真实的中国，其实变化非常快。

如今在中国很多城市：

  • 手机支付已经成为日常
  • 高铁连接着绝大多数大型城市
  • 商场和酒店开始出现配送机器人
  • AI 工具逐渐进入普通人的生活
  • 新能源汽车越来越普遍
  • 城市每年都在变得更智能

与此同时，传统的街边生活、人情味与本地文化，却依然保留着。

这种“未来感”与“烟火气”同时存在的状态，让我非常着迷。

所以，我想以一个真正生活在这里的普通人的视角，去记录这些变化。

不是为了宣传什么。

只是想让更多人看到真实的中国。

这个网站会写什么

这个网站主要围绕两个方向展开。

1、 中国旅行与城市文化

这里会分享：

  • 值得旅行的中国城市
  • 重庆本地生活
  • 城市夜景与摄影
  • 中国街头文化
  • 很多游客看不到的真实角落
  • 以本地人视角观察到的旅行体验

相比传统旅游攻略，我更关注：

一座城市真实的气质。

它深夜里的状态。
普通人的生活方式。
街边的小店。
城市的声音与节奏。

因为这些细节，才是真正构成一座城市灵魂的东西。

2、 中国 AI、机器人与未来科技

作为曾经的软件工程师，我也长期关注中国 AI 与科技的发展。

这里会涉及：

  • 中国 AI 发展
  • 机器人与科技创新
  • 智能交通与无人驾驶
  • 数字化生活
  • 科技如何改变旅行与日常生活

但我不会用复杂难懂的专业术语。

我更想以普通人的视角，去观察科技如何真正进入现实生活。

3. 世界热点事件分享与个人见解

我持续关注全球各地实时热点、国际时事与跨境资讯。
除科技、旅行内容外，我会整理全网热门国际事件，用通俗语言梳理事件完整脉络，同时输出客观理性的个人观点与独立思考。
深挖事件背后的成因、发展走向以及长远影响，秉持中立包容的态度看待多方声音，帮助海外读者全面了解世界局势，建立多元、理性的全球视野。

我眼中的中国

如今的中国，很难用单一标签去形容。

这里既古老，又未来。

你可能刚经过一条拥有几百年历史的小巷。
转身又进入一座布满机器人与智能系统的现代商场。

传统与未来，在这里同时存在。

而我觉得，海外很多人其实还没有真正看到这一面的中国。

这也是我持续写下去的原因。

最后

我不希望这个网站像官方宣传。

我更希望它是真实的、个人化的、有温度的。

如果你也对这些内容感兴趣：

  • 中国旅行
  • 赛博朋克城市
  • AI 与未来科技
  • 城市摄影
  • 中国现代生活
  • 中国城市文化

那么欢迎来到这里。

希望你能在这里，看到一个与你印象中不太一样的中国。

“真实的故事，永远比刻板印象更有力量。”
